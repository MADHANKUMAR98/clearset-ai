# Skill: `recommend_resolution`

## Execution Steps
1. Match root cause with applicable SOP section.
2. Formulate ordered list of operational actions (SWIFT repair dispatch, escalation, continuous monitoring).
3. Compute potential CSDR penalty and risk avoidance metrics.
4. Prepare Human Approval package with [Approve], [Reject], and [Request More Evidence] options.
